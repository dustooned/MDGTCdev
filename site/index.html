Meet Dustin ↗

I started Mr. Duck Goes to College to share what it feels like to be a college student: finding your way, meeting people, making choices, and figuring things out as you go.

I’m a Cuban-Nicaragüense artist and educator. My background brings together comics, animation, design, and games. Teaching gives that work a shared purpose: helping students find their voice and see what they can make.

Try an idea here. Take it into real life.

I want this game to give students room to explore problems and try solutions through play. A virtual world can be a place to ask questions, see what changes, and imagine how things could be better. My hope is that those small discoveries inspire action beyond the screen.

This is a homegrown, community collaboration taking shape at Long Beach City College. It’s also an ongoing experiment in teaching: bringing digital media, game technology, and the social sciences into the same creative space. As it grows, the goal is to make room for students and collaborators to contribute art, stories, sound, research, and ideas.

Before you play

A desktop browser works best. Click inside the game to enable sound. Move with WASD or the arrows, use E to talk, and Esc to pause. A controller works too.

Things are still changing, and some art is temporary. After you play, the feedback page walks you through sharing what you noticed. You can also download a feedback template.
