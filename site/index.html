A few things to try

Follow the objective at the top right: Eli is in the campus admin court, Nora is on the downtown Common Ground plaza. Choices float as thought clouds; each one shows its time cost before anything happens. There is no deadline.

Talk to the neighbors on the sidewalks. Most have a complaint about the city before their tip. Rosa waits at the downtown bus stop and speaks Spanish; try her before and after Eli's greeting. Nora keeps seed-favor hours, 9:00 to 18:00; if you miss her, the favor waits for another day, and she remembers when you have helped before. Skating, jumps and tricks still use simple placeholder poses.

Mr. Duck has new eyes: big, hand-drawn and a bit googly. He rides goofy now, standing on his board instead of through it, kicks to push, and his eyes react to each trick, bail and landing.

Conversations read more naturally now. Eli reacts once you learn the greeting, the seed handoff opens straight on the seeds, and every choice card asks plainly (â€œPractice a Spanish greeting?â€) with Yes or Not now. Some doors now open. Look for a gold mat: the administration offices on campus, the four downtown shops, the Pixel Parlor arcade (its cabinets are just for looking, for now) and two courtyard houses. Step inside with E; walk back out the door to leave. The rooms are placeholders for now, so nobody is home yet.

The Common Ground vent wheezes. Borrow a fan at the campus equipment desk, learn to aim it at the airflow bench, then turn it to fit the plaza vent and run it on low or boost. Or ask the neighbors to help; Nora will thank you for whichever you did.

Sprint or push hard and a stamina wheel appears beside Mr. Duck. It drains as you go and fades once it refills; the gold dots under it are your fitness level. The screen otherwise stays clear: the clock, your objective and a prompt when there is something to do. Press F3 (or use Pause) for the old debug readouts.
