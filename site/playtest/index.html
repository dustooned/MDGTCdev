Mr. Duck Playtest

Thanks for playing! It takes about 30 to 40 minutes: play one day in Mr. Duck Goes to College, then answer a few questions. There are no wrong answers. If something confused you, that is exactly what we want to hear.

Step 1

Before you play

  • Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Turn the sound on if you can.
  • Playing with a controller? Plug it in before opening the game, then press a button once it loads.
  • On the title card, choose Start a new test day so you begin fresh. It only clears a test day saved in this browser.
  • Note the time you start. If someone is watching, try saying what you're thinking as you play.
  • Keep this page open for the controls below. Try not to read the questions until you've played.

Step 2

Play one day

You start in Mr. Duck's apartment hall. Downtown is downhill and the college is up the greenway. Do whatever interests you, in any order. Nothing can go badly wrong and there's no deadline. Afterwards, tick everything you did. You don't need to do them all.

Step 3 · only if you used a controller

Controller checks

For each line, pick Worked, Trouble or Didn't try. If you mark Trouble, say what happened in the box below.
